The Multi-Channel Blind Spot

The Multi-Desk Visibility Gap

The India Today Group operates diverse channels—from Aaj Tak and India Today TV to regional feeds and digital arms. Without centralized telemetry, leadership lacks a unified window into story status across broadcast and social platforms.

Channel Isolation

Each channel operates in its own workflow ecosystem. Senior editors cannot verify at a glance whether a breaking story feed has been cleared, edited, or pushed to social desks.

Social Syndication Lag

Stories air on television long before clips are custom-cut for Twitter, Facebook, or Instagram Reels due to manual coordination bottlenecks and lack of stage visibility.
